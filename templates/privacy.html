Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense data you choose to log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the Service — this includes storing your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and authenticating your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers to host the Service. These providers process data solely on our behalf and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with any other third parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
